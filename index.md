# Rearview privacy policy

Effective: November 16, 2026

Rearview is a vehicle log. This policy explains what happens to the
information you put in it.

## Your log stays on your phone

Vehicles, fill-ups, services, expenses, photos and settings are stored only on
your device. Rearview has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it
does not send your log to us or to anyone else.

Your log leaves the device only when you choose to: when you export a file and
share or save it with another app, or when your phone's own backup copies app
data under your system settings.

Choosing a vehicle photo uses the system photo picker, which gives Rearview
only the photo you pick, not access to your library.

## App updates

To check for updates to the app, Rearview contacts Expo (expo.dev), the
service that delivers them. Each check sends:

- a random identifier created when the app is installed, which is not linked
  to you, your account or your device's advertising ID;
- the platform (Android or iOS) and which version of the app is installed;
- if an update fails to start, the error message, so the app can go back to a
  working version.

The connection is encrypted. Expo processes this information to deliver
updates under its own privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Rearview, or using Settings → Erase all data, deletes your log from
the device. Because we don't receive your log, there is nothing for us to
delete.

## Children

Rearview is not directed at children under 13.
